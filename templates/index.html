Whether you are a student choosing between backend, frontend, AI, or DevOps paths — or a professional switching careers — the analyzer turns a broad goal into a realistic, step-by-step learning plan.

Frequently asked questions

What is a skill gap analysis?

A skill gap analysis compares your existing skills to the requirements of a target role. It reveals which competencies you already have, which are missing, and how to close those gaps efficiently.

Can I analyze a custom role?

Yes. Choose a preset role or type any custom role — for example AI Engineer, DevOps Lead, Cloud Architect, Data Engineer, or Cybersecurity Specialist.

Is the analysis private?

Yes. Analyses are saved only to your account and can be deleted at any time. Auto-cleanup removes analyses older than 30 days unless you choose to keep them permanently.

Can I export my report?

Yes. You can export a professional PDF report or a DOCX document with the full roadmap, metrics, and recommendations.

Does this help with portfolio projects?

Yes. Each analysis includes custom portfolio project blueprints that combine your existing skills with the new ones you need to build, giving you practical, interview-ready evidence.
